# Play Store listing copy

Paste-ready text for the Play Console listing, with character counts measured
against Play's limits rather than estimated. Regenerate the counts by measuring,
not by eye, if any of this is edited.

This app is an internal staff tool served with `robots: noindex`. The copy has
no SEO job at all. It is read by exactly two people: a Play reviewer deciding
what the app is, and a rep who was told to install it. Nothing here is written
for a search engine, and there is no keyword stuffing to strip out later.

Whether the listing is localised into Greek at all is a Part C decision. If it
is not, the English column ships alone and the Greek stays here for when it is.

### Full description

**English** (1729/4000)

```
International Rentals is a rent-a-car company in Crete. This app is the tool its own staff use to run the day. It is not a booking site, it sells nothing, and it has no public sign-up.

A rep works from a hotel desk, on a phone, with a guest standing in front of them. The app answers the three questions that matter in that moment.

IS THERE A CAR?
Availability shows what is genuinely free for a set of dates, by category. A car that is booked, out, or off the road for service is simply not offered.

WHAT DOES IT COST?
The price comes from the company's own table, on the server. It is worked out for the dates in front of the rep and handed back as one number. It is not negotiated at the desk and it cannot be discounted there.

CAN THIS PERSON DRIVE IT?
Age and licence checks run before a car leaves. A failed check stops the pickup. Only the manager can override one, and the override is recorded.

PICKUP AND RETURN, WITHOUT THE PAPER
The driving licence is photographed and read, existing damage is marked on a diagram of the car, fuel is recorded, and the customer signs on the screen. A PDF rental agreement is produced and sent to them. On return, fuel and any new damage are recorded against the same booking.

FOR THE MANAGER
The day's movements, the fleet, the price tables, the cash each rep has collected and handed in, and an audit trail of every change made by anyone.

Greek and English throughout, switchable per person. The app records cash taken at the desk. It does not process payments, it carries no advertising, and it collects nothing for anybody other than the company itself.

Access is by invitation only. Accounts are created by the company for its own staff, and every screen is behind a login.
```

## Two answers that are not free text, but belong with the copy

**App access.** Every screen in this app is behind a login, so a reviewer who
is handed no credentials sees a login wall and rejects the submission. The
field needs a working demo account against the listing seed, plus a sentence
saying the app is an internal staff tool with no public signup. This is Part C
item 8 and it is the single most common reason a submission like this bounces.

**Data safety.** Prepared separately, in `assets/play/data-safety.md`. Do not
answer it from this file.
